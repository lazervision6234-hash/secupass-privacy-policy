SECUPASS Password Manager Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. Introduction

SECUPASS Password Manager helps users store and manage passwords on their devices.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Password information entered into the app is intended to be stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers, provided the app operates as described.

3. Advertising and Sharing

SECUPASS does not display advertisements. We do not sell users' stored password information.

4. Premium Purchases

If premium features are purchased through Google Play, Google Play processes the transaction under its own terms and privacy policy.

5. Security

Please protect your device with a secure screen lock. No electronic storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Data Deletion

Locally stored data may be deleted using app features or by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. Back up information you need to retain before doing so.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this policy when our app or data practices change.
